{%- comment -%} WHAT THIS BLOCK IS FOR. Every page on this site used to render {{ site.title }}, so all 52 posts told a search engine they were the same document, called "Akash Hadagali | Founder, Engineer, Builder". The title is the strongest thing a page says about itself and not one post was saying anything. There was no canonical either, so the 22 posts that also live on onemana.dev were two unlabelled copies competing with each other, and no og: or twitter: tags, so every share on the channel that actually sends traffic rendered as a bare link. page.canonical_url is set on a post that was republished elsewhere and names the copy that should win.

If you're new here: [OneCamp](https://onemana.dev) is a self-hosted workspace (chat, tasks, docs, calls, calendar and AI agents) that you install on your own server. It is [open source](https://github.com/OneMana-Soft/OneCamp) and free for up to 25 people.

**If you run OneCamp, update today, with AI or without.** Today's update is a security fix, and it's the only change: emails now show what people wrote as plain text. A message, a comment, a task's name or an event's notes reach an email exactly as they were typed. There's nothing to migrate and no setting to change, and everyone stays signed in. To update a server you run, follow the steps under Updating on the [installation page](https://onemana.dev/docs/installation#updating). Everything else that's new is in the [last post](https://onemana.dev/blog/outlook-calendar-project-releases-and-calls-that-ring).

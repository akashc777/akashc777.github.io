If you're new here: [OneCamp](https://onemana.dev) is a self-hosted workspace (chat, tasks, docs, calls, calendar and AI agents) that you install on your own server. It is [open source](https://github.com/OneMana-Soft/OneCamp) and free for up to 25 people.

Some things take a paragraph to type and ten seconds to say. Some take a paragraph to type and one look at your screen to show. A call is too much for either. That's what clips are for, and OneCamp has them now.

![Recording a voice clip in a OneCamp channel](/assets/images/post/onecamp-clips.jpg)

## Three kinds

Press the microphone beside the paperclip in any message box (a channel, a DM, a group chat or a thread) and choose:

- **Voice**: a voice note.
- **Video**: you, on camera.
- **Screen**: a screen, window or tab, with your voice over it. Walk someone through a bug or a design without booking a call.

Record for up to five minutes. **Pause** when you need to, then **Stop**, play it back, and **Attach clip** or **Record again**. It's attached to your message like any file, so you can add a line of text and send them together. Nothing leaves your browser until you send it.

## Good to know

- A clip is a file like any other, so it lives on your server and follows your workspace's upload limit for a file. The recorder shows how much time is left and stops at the limit; an admin can raise it in **Admin → General**.
- Voice clips play as audio, video and screen clips as video, right in the conversation.
- Your camera and microphone are released the moment the recorder closes.

## How it compares

[Slack](https://slack.com/help/articles/4406235165587-Record-audio-and-video-clips-in-Slack) has clips of up to five minutes on every plan, and [Teams raised its video clips](https://mc.merill.net/message/MC1066938) from one minute to five in 2025. OneCamp's clips match them, and stay on your own server, with no seat to pay for.

## Try it

- [Open the live demo](https://onecamp.onemana.dev/?start_demo=1), go to **#design**, and press the microphone in the message box.
- On your own server: [onemana.dev/free](https://onemana.dev/free) gives you the install command, no email needed.
- Already running OneCamp? Update to v2.67.0 (with AI) or v1.52.0 (without AI).
